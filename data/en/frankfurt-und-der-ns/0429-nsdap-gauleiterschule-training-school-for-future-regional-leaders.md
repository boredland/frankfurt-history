# NSDAP-Gauleit­erschule (trai­ning school for future regio­nal lea­ders)

*Niederräder Landstraße 10*

![Picture postcard with exterior view of the Jakob Sprenger School](../../images/C80410_jpg_352d9bf86f0049bbc5cd492da7368f82_original.jpg)
*1938 — Author: unbekannt | License: HMF, Sammlung Church, C80410 | Description: Picture postcard with exterior view of the Jakob Sprenger School*

From 1935, this was the location of a training school for future regional leaders (“Gauleiter”) of the National Socialist German Workers’ Party (NSDAP). The school, named after Jakob Sprenger, Hesse-Nassau’s regional leader, was located in the residence of the Gerst family which owned the Tietz chain of department stores. After the Nazi Party assumed power, the family was harassed and bullied, and left financially ruined by the boycott of their stores. The family’s town house was also confiscated.
