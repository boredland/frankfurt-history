University chapter of the NSDAP, Nazi Women's League and Nazi Factory Cell Origanization (NSBO)

A glimpse inside the 1935 directory shows that the university chapter of the National Socialist German Workers’ Party as well as the Nazi Women’s League and the Nazi Factory Cell Organization had their offices here.
