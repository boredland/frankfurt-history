# Kinematographentheater

*1907-1914, 77 Kaiserstraße*

![Programme listing of cinematograph theatres in the journal Der Kinematograph, March 1907.](../../images/f5ac61cc6a2bfeb919f756226c60497c_original.jpg)
*1907 — Author: unbekannt/unkown | License: Kinematograph Nr.10, März 1907 | Description: Programme listing of cinematograph theatres in the journal Der Kinematograph, March 1907.*

Frankfurt’s second cinema – the Kinematographentheater

For a short time, a cinema operated here at Kaiserstraße 77. Unfortunately, little is known about it today. It was opened in late 1906 or early 1907 by August Haslwanter. Since he already launched Frankfurt’s first cinema the year before (→ Kinematographentheater Kaiserstraße 60), he now had two cinemas in the city – and Frankfurt had three cinemas in total.

Established in a building formerly housing the Seip Musikwerke, Halswanter’s cinema here could seat around 210 people. Until this venue closed in 1914, Haslwanter – an ingenious entrepreneur –usually ran the same program in both his cinemas.

The building itself, designed by architect Eugen Greiß and constructed in around 1897, was torn down in 2006. However, the original neo-baroque sandstone façade was retained for the new structure on the site. Today, it is one of the protected monuments in Frankfurt’s Main Station neighborhood.

*Author: Clara Itterbeck und Carla Sternburg*
